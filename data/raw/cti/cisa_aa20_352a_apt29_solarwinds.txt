NARRATIVE:
In December 2020, CISA released Alert AA20-352A regarding an Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) actor attributed as APT29 / Nobelium / Cozy Bear conducting a global supply chain compromise via SolarWinds Orion. The threat actor inserted a malicious backdoor known as SUNBURST (Solorigate) into Orion platform software builds. Upon execution, SUNBURST stayed dormant for up to two weeks before resolving primary command-and-control (C2) domains including avsvmcloud[.]com, using dynamically constructed DGA subdomains to exfiltrate host reconnaissance. Secondary stagers including TEARDROP and Raindrop memory-only droppers communicated with C2 IP 185.225.17.42 and backup infrastructure freescanonline[.]com (hosted at 20.140.8.11). APT29 systematically exploited Exchange vulnerabilities (CVE-2021-26855 ProxyLogon) for lateral movement and forged SAML tokens to compromise cloud identities (MITRE ATT&CK T1195.002 Supply Chain Compromise and T1078 Valid Accounts).
