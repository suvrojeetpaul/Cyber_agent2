NARRATIVE:
Joint Advisory CISA AA24-038A details PRC state-sponsored group Volt Typhoon compromising critical infrastructure sectors across the United States (communications, energy, transportation, water). Volt Typhoon emphasizes living-off-the-land (LotL) techniques, abusing built-in Windows admin binaries (wmic, netsh, ntdsutil) rather than traditional malware. To build resilient proxy networks, the group deploys the KV-botnet and Fast Reverse Proxy (FRP) across end-of-life SOHO routers routing through 198.51.100.44 and 203.0.113.88 with dynamic domains like cisco-gateway-mesh[.]org. The actor exploits FortiOS SSL-VPN vulnerability CVE-2023-27997 for edge appliance infiltration and maintains years-long persistence via stolen administrator credentials (MITRE ATT&CK T1078 Valid Accounts and T1572 Protocol Tunneling).
